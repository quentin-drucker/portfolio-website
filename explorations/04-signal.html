Computational World‑Building

A wabi-sabi-inspired system for generative worlds. A still photograph does not get rebuilt in 3D. It gets read, and what's read steers how a world grows.

Brightness, color, contrast, density and texture become a small set of legible parameters. Unreal Engine, programmed in C++, uses them to bias terrain, scattering, weathering and atmosphere toward asymmetry and controlled disorder.

Predecessor: a UE5 PCG forest prototype with ambient sound, fog and first-person exploration.
