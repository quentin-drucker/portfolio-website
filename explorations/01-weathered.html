Computational World-Building

A wabi-sabi-inspired system for generative worlds, in which a still photograph shapes an explorable environment without being copied by it.

Brightness, color, contrast, density and texture are read from an image and translated into a small set of understandable parameters. Those parameters bias terrain, scattering, weathering and atmosphere in Unreal Engine, built in C++, toward asymmetry, irregularity and controlled disorder.

  1. photograph

  4. terrain

Builds on an earlier UE5 prototype: a PCG forest with ambient sound, fog and first-person exploration.
