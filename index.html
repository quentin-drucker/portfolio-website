5 About

Curious about how machines can see, reason, and create.

I’m a Computer Science major with a minor in Art at Connecticut College and an Ammerman Center for Arts and Technology Scholar.

I’m particularly interested in visual computing, with interests spanning both computer graphics and computer vision. My passion for this area began at the intersection of technology and visual art and has expanded into a broader interest in how computing can be applied both to the generation of visual content and to the analysis and interpretation of visual information.

I’m always eager to learn and to collaborate on projects that blend innovation with functionality, bringing together technical depth, creativity, and thoughtful design. That spirit extends across the stack — from algorithms and research pipelines to interfaces, 3D environments, and real-time systems.
